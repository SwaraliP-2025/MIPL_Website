## 🎨 Design Improvements

### Color Palette
- Primary: Keep current blue (#667eea)
- Add: Professional gray tones
- Add: Accent colors for CTAs
- Ensure WCAG AA contrast ratios

### Typography
- Headings: Larger, bolder
- Body: More line-height for readability
- Add: Pull quotes for testimonials
- Consistent hierarchy

### Spacing
- More white space between sections
- Consistent padding/margins
- Breathing room around content

### Animations
- Scroll-triggered fade-ins
- Number counters
- Hover effects on cards
- Smooth page transitions (already have ✅)
